# Functions and Lambda Experiment

This folder contains 52 question-wise Python programs. Files Q1-Q32 cover functions, recursion, and function arguments. Files Q33-Q52 cover lambda expressions with `map`, `filter`, and sorting.

Run a program from this folder with `python q01_factorial.py`, replacing the filename with the question you want to run.

The electricity examples use progressive rates of 1.50 per unit for the first 100 units, 2.50 for the next 100, and 4.00 for additional units. The modular bill adds a fixed charge of 50, tax of 5%, and a 10% discount on energy charges below 100 units. The salary example uses HRA of 20% and DA of 10%. The item bill applies a 10% discount when the subtotal is at least 5000. Grade thresholds are A at 90%, B at 80%, C at 70%, D at 60%, and F below 60%. The patient and product invoice examples use sample discounts and tax rates defined in their programs.
